Mu 10 is the Mu. Burgerhouse location on 10 Avenida Norte at Calle 24, in the Quintas del Carmen neighbourhood of Playa del Carmen, a few blocks from Quinta Avenida. You get the full Mu menu here: smash, classic cut and wagyu burgers with fries included, the Big Mu Cheese Burger, beef hot dogs, starters to share, 300 g orders and the Margarita & Mezcalita Bar with cocktails, craft beer and Mexican wine.

Open every day from 1:30 pm to 11:30 pm, with no break between lunch and dinner. By car, 10 Avenida runs parallel to Quinta Avenida two blocks inland; on foot it is five minutes from Quinta along Calle 24. [CONFIRM] parking.

It is for a slow meal after the beach, for dinner with friends or as a couple, and for families with kids who want a real burger. If you are visiting, it is the location closest to the tourist area. There are beef-free options too: the Olvídate de mí with Beyond Meat (+20) and the No carne, no problem.

FAQ

What are Mu 10's hours?

Every day from 1:30 pm to 11:30 pm.

Can I book a table?

Message us on WhatsApp at +52 984 179 2682 (the same number for all three locations). [CONFIRM] reservation and group policy.

Do you deliver?

Yes, Mu 10 is on Rappi and Uber Eats; the direct links are above on this page.

How do I get an invoice (CFDI)?

Ask the captain at the branch where you dined, before you leave, with your receipt and tax details.

Are there meat-free options?

Yes: the Olvídate de mí is made with Beyond Meat (plant-based, +20) and the No carne, no problem is the meat-free burger on the menu. Ask the captain about the other ingredients.
