Mu 40 is the Mu. Burgerhouse location in downtown Playa del Carmen: 40 Avenida Norte 11, between Calle 4 and Calle 6. It has the most reviews of the three and it is the only one with the Av. 40 exclusives, such as the green & yellow lemon pie. The menu is the usual one: smash, classic cut and wagyu burgers with fries included, the Big Mu Cheese Burger, hot dogs, starters, 300 g orders and the Margarita & Mezcalita Bar.

Open Tuesday to Sunday: Tuesday, Wednesday and Sunday from 2 pm to 10 pm; Thursday, Friday and Saturday from 2 pm to 11 pm. Closed on Mondays. 40 Avenida is the main avenue across downtown; the entrance is between Calle 4 and Calle 6, on the Avenida Juárez side. Street parking on 40 Avenida.

It is the locals' branch: office lunch, weekday dinner and Thursday-to-Saturday nights with cocktails. If you do not eat beef, there are the Olvídate de mí with Beyond Meat (+20) and the No carne, no problem.

FAQ

What are Mu 40's hours?

Tuesday, Wednesday and Sunday 2–10 pm; Thursday to Saturday 2–11 pm. Closed Mondays.

Can I book a table?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp at +52 984 179 2682 (the same number for all three locations) and we will confirm. For large groups, please let us know a day in advance.

Do you deliver?

Yes, Mu 40 is on Rappi and Uber Eats; the direct links are above on this page.

How do I get an invoice (CFDI)?

Ask the captain at the branch where you dined, before you leave, with your receipt and tax details.

Are there meat-free options?

Yes: the Olvídate de mí is made with Beyond Meat (plant-based, +20) and the No carne, no problem is the meat-free burger on the menu. Ask the captain about the other ingredients.
